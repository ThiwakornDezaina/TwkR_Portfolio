Regulation Library (RLS)_

An internal policy-document library for EGAT, built around fast search and role-based management.

Role: UI/UX Design Client: EGAT Year: 2023
rls.egat.co.th
01

Problem

The client requested a redesign of the existing Regulation Library system to make the management of regulations, policies, and internal documents more structured and efficient.

The system needed to provide an improved Management experience for Admins to add, edit, and update regulatory content. Any newly added or modified content had to go through an approval process before being published to the system.

For Users, the system provides access to key regulatory content, including Regulations, Consultation Records, EGAT Forms, Approval Authorities, Regulatory News, Infographics, and User Manuals. Admins also have additional management functions, including content management and log records.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research, Prototype
CLIENTEGAT

02

Goal

To create a more structured and manageable system for handling regulations, policies, and internal documents—from content management and approval to publication and user access.

For Users, the goal was to make regulatory information easier to search, navigate, and read, while providing clear visibility into previous versions and their current status.

03

Challenge

The main challenge was designing a multi-role system with different permissions and responsibilities. In particular, the Admin workflow required regulatory content to go through an approval process before it could be published, making clear workflow and status communication essential.

Another challenge was organizing multiple types of regulatory content and managing previous versions of documents. The interface needed to clearly distinguish current content from cancelled or previous versions without creating confusion for Users.

04

Design Approach

I redesigned the UX/UI by separating the experience according to different user roles and designing a structured management flow for Admins. The flow covers adding, editing, and updating content, as well as submitting changes for approval before publication.

For Users, I designed Search and Filter functions to make regulatory documents easier to find and access. I also introduced shortcuts for navigating between sections of long-form content, allowing users to quickly jump to specific topics.

For regulatory content with previous versions, I designed the information structure to display earlier versions with a clear “Cancelled” status. This allows users to distinguish between current and previous versions while maintaining access to historical regulatory information.
