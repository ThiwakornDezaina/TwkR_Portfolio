Regulation Library (RLS)_

An internal policy-document library for EGAT, built around fast search and role-based management.

Role: UI/UX Design Client: EGAT Year: 2023
01

Overview

EGAT needed an internal library where staff could find the right regulation quickly instead of digging through folders. The design leads with a single search bar and a grid of plain-language topic categories — expenses, welfare, procurement, vehicles, business, land, and more — so people can jump straight to the right document type.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research, Prototype
CLIENTEGAT
YEAR2023
PLATFORMWeb
02

What I Designed

  • A search-first homepage with a prominent search bar above categorized topic filters
  • Distinct flows for User, Admin, and Super Admin roles with different levels of access
  • A straightforward content-management view so non-technical admin staff can publish and update documents

Reflection
