SomeBank_

An internal dashboard concept for a banking team, surfacing task status, sales trends, and training progress on a single home screen.

Role: UI/UX Design, Prototype Year: 2024 Platform: Web (Admin)
01

Client's Request

We collaborated with internal staff at the bank to address their need for a centralized system that could streamline daily operations and improve workflow efficiency.

The project began with a focus on the loan request and data management process, which was previously disorganized, time-consuming, and lacked a formal system for communication and documentation.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research, Prototype
YEAR2024
PLATFORMWeb (Admin Dashboard)
TOOLSFigma
02

The Solution

We designed a centralized platform to replace scattered task documentation with a structured, user-friendly system — starting with loan processing.

03

Challenge

Transitioning staff from familiar social messaging tools to a formal system required balancing structure with usability, addressing inconsistent processes, and ensuring smooth cross-department adoption.

04

Goal

Our goal was to reduce processing time, minimize communication errors, improve data traceability, and ultimately enhance staff satisfaction through a user-friendly experience.
