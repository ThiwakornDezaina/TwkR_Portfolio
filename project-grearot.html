Smart Farming & Farm Management Platform

Role: UI/UX Design Year: 2022 Platform: Mobile Web
grearot.app
01

Problem

The client wanted to develop a mobile-first web application that helps users manage and monitor their farming activities in one place.

The platform needed to support different types of growing environments, including gardens, fields, farms, and greenhouses. It also needed to integrate with IoT tracking devices provided by InnoBean, allowing users to monitor their growing environment and farming activities through the platform.

In addition to farm management and tracking, the platform was designed to provide users with relevant cultivation knowledge and guidance when they encounter common growing problems. A content feed on the homepage would also provide educational information and updates related to plant cultivation.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research
YEAR2022
PLATFORMMobile Web

02

Goal

To create a centralized farming experience that helps users manage their growing areas, monitor cultivation activities, and access relevant knowledge in one place.

The goal was also to make farming information more accessible by combining farm management, IoT-based tracking, and educational content into a simple mobile-first experience.

03

Challenge

The main challenge was designing a single experience that could support different types of growing environments while keeping the user journey simple and understandable.

The platform also combined several different functions—farm management, IoT tracking, cultivation activities, and educational content—requiring the information architecture and user flow to connect these areas without making the experience feel overly complex.

Another challenge was designing guidance around potential cultivation problems so that users could easily move from identifying an issue to finding relevant information that could help them address it.

04

Design Approach

I designed the UX/UI for the mobile-first web application, focusing on the core user journey from onboarding and setting up a growing area to managing cultivation activities.

The experience allows users to select the type of growing environment they manage, such as a garden, field, farm, or greenhouse, and then manage the corresponding cultivation activities. I also incorporated the concept of IoT-based tracking into the experience to support monitoring through InnoBean's connected devices.

To support users beyond basic farm management, I designed educational content and problem-solving guidance as part of the experience. For example, when users encounter issues with their plants, the platform can provide relevant cultivation knowledge to help them understand and address the problem.

The homepage also includes a cultivation-focused content feed, allowing users to learn and discover useful information while using the platform.
