A mobile-first concept built around news and resources for Thai farmers.

Role: UI/UX Design Year: 2022 Platform: Mobile Web
grearot.app
01

Overview

Grearot (“the next step forward”) is a mobile website concept for Thai agriculture, centered on a news-and-resource feed — planting tips, crop updates, and community content — rather than a transactional storefront.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research
YEAR2022
PLATFORMMobile Web
TOOLSFigma
02

What I Designed

  • A green-branded splash and home screen built mobile-first for on-the-go reading
  • A news feed split into “today's news” and “recommended articles,” each with a short excerpt and thumbnail
  • A lightweight, scannable card layout suited to quick daily check-ins rather than long reading sessions

Reflection

The brief was less about selling something and more about giving farmers a reason to open the app daily — so the feed format took priority over deeper e-commerce features.
