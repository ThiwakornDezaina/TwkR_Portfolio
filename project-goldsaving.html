Corporate Digital Gold Saving_

A mobile gold-saving platform for corporate members — balances, contributions, and live price tracking in one place.

Role: UI/UX Design Client: Saha Pathanapibul Year: 2023
goldsaving.app
01

Problem

The client wanted to develop a web application for managing and trading digital gold within the organization. The platform allows employees to receive gold as rewards, purchase and sell gold independently, monitor real-time gold prices, and manage their gold holdings.

The experience was designed primarily for Mobile Web, with users accessing the platform through LINE OpenChat and completing OTP verification before entering the system.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research
CLIENTSaha Pathanapibul
YEAR2023
PLATFORMMobile Web
02

Goal

The goal was to create an experience that enables employees to independently manage the gold they receive from the organization — from checking their balance and choosing whether to hold or sell their gold, to transferring gold to others with ease.

At the same time, the experience needed to build user confidence during transactions by presenting information clearly, providing appropriate identity verification, and designing transaction flows that minimize the risk of errors.

03

Challenge

The main challenge was designing user flows involving personal assets and financial transactions, where accuracy and clarity are critical at every step.

The platform also relies on real-time gold prices and includes security requirements such as OTP verification and QR Code-based gold transfers. The challenge was to make these flows easy to understand while maintaining security and reducing the possibility of user errors during transactions.

In addition, the platform was designed specifically for Mobile Web and required users to start their journey through LINE OpenChat. This meant creating a seamless experience from authentication and onboarding through to the core gold management and transaction flows.

04

Design Approach

I was responsible for the initial UX/UI design phase of the project, covering User Flows, Wireframes, and High-Fidelity UI Design. My main focus was on the authentication and onboarding experience, as well as the core experience for managing digital gold.

I designed the homepage as a central hub where users could easily access key information, including real-time gold prices, gold balance, cash balance, and transaction history.

I also designed flows for managing gold across different actions, including holding, selling, and transferring gold.

For the gold transfer experience, I designed a QR Code-based gold receiving flow, with an additional OTP verification step before completing the transaction. This was intended to make the process clear and intuitive while providing an additional layer of security for asset-related transactions.

After the initial phase of the project was completed, the subsequent design work was handed over to another designer.
