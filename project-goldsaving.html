Corporate Digital Gold Saving_

A mobile gold-saving platform for corporate members — balances, contributions, and live price tracking in one place.

Role: UI/UX Design Client: Saha Pathanapibul Year: 2023
goldsaving.app
01

Overview

Designed for Saha Pathanapibul through Double P Enterprise, this platform lets corporate members track gold holdings, buy and sell, and follow live prices without needing prior familiarity with gold-trading products. The home screen leads with today's buy and sell prices for both 96.5% and 99.99% purity gold, followed by current holdings shown in both weight and cash value.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research
CLIENTSaha Pathanapibul
YEAR2023
PLATFORMMobile App
02

What I Designed

  • A price-first home screen showing today's buy/sell rates before anything else, since that's what most members check first
  • Holdings broken out by gold purity (96.5% / 99.99%) with both weight and cash-equivalent value shown together
  • An identity-verification flow — personal info, phone number, ID photo, and a selfie with ID — for account activation

Reflection

Financial products like this live or die on trust — the priority was making sure numbers (prices, holdings, balances) were never ambiguous, especially for members who don't trade gold regularly.
