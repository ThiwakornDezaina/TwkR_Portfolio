VR Seeker_

A land and property listings platform, designed around how people search for and compare land.

Role: UI/UX Design Client: Double P Enterprise Year: 2023
vrseeker.co.th
01

Overview

VR Seeker is a land-listing service — the homepage leads with a large search bar over a map-like hero image, letting visitors search by location, price, or property type before browsing curated and promoted listings below.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research, Prototype
CLIENTDouble P Enterprise
YEAR2023
PLATFORMWeb
02

What I Designed

  • A search-first hero combining location search with property-type filtering
  • A featured-listings grid surfacing price, size, and location at a glance
  • A separate promoted-listings section with discounted pricing clearly marked
03

04

Reflection

Land listings involve a lot of dense information — price, size, location, ownership type. The design tries to keep the browsing view scannable and push detailed comparison to the individual listing page.
