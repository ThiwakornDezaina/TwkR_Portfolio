VR Seeker_

A land and property listings platform, designed around how people search for and compare land.

Role: UI/UX Design Client: Double P Enterprise Year: 2023
vrseeker.co.th
01

Problem

The client wanted to transform an existing property information process, which was previously managed through Excel files, into a structured real estate website.

The platform needed to organize properties by category and present property information in a clear and easy-to-understand format. It also needed to provide location information through maps, along with Search and Filter functions to help users find relevant properties more easily.

The primary users are prospective property buyers and sellers. For properties managed by an agent, users should also be able to contact the responsible agent directly.

ROLEUI Design, UX Design, UX Research, Prototype
CLIENTDouble P Enterprise
YEAR2023
PLATFORMWeb
02

Goal

To transform fragmented, Excel-based property information into an accessible and structured real estate platform that makes it easier for buyers and sellers to discover and understand available properties.

The experience aims to help users efficiently search for relevant properties, understand their location and surrounding context, and connect with the responsible agent when further information is required.

03

Challenge

The main challenge was transforming property information from Excel files into a user-friendly digital experience while organizing a wide range of properties into clear categories.

The design also needed to balance multiple information needs—including property details, location, Search and Filter functions, and agent contact—without overwhelming users. The challenge was to structure these elements in a way that allowed both buyers and sellers to navigate and understand the information easily.

04

Design Approach

I redesigned the experience by transforming the existing Excel-based property information into a structured web experience. I designed the information architecture and page layouts to organize properties by real estate category and make key property information easier to scan and understand.

I designed Wireframes and UI for the property browsing experience, incorporating Search and Filter functions to help users narrow down relevant listings. Location and Map information were also integrated into the experience to provide users with better context about each property and its surrounding area.

For properties managed by an agent, I designed a direct contact path so users could easily connect with the responsible agent.
